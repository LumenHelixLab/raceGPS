# Post-edit overlays: raceGPS Cleveland cinematic (5 × 10 s scenes, about 50 s)

**Status: draft plan.** The video has not been produced yet.

All on-screen words go in during the edit (DaVinci Resolve), timed to the streaks and the empty glowing panels in each generated clip. The scene prompts ask for "no readable text" on purpose, because video models garble lettering.

## Style for every word
- Midnight Club-style game art: bold condensed italic caps, a fast slide or glitch-in on the streak, a 2–3 frame white flash on the hit, and a glitch or wipe on the way out.
- Colors: raceGPS red as the main color, with purple, green and blue cyber accents (outer glow, chromatic split).
- Each callout sits inside the empty glowing panel that its scene creates at 0:00–0:02.
